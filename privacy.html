Privacy

Privacy policy.

Sweet Beans Grooming is a small, owner-operated business in Seattle. This page explains what information we collect, why, and who can see it. Last updated September 25, 2026.

What we collect. When you book, we collect your name, email address, and phone number, and details about your pet such as name, breed, age, veterinarian, coat and health notes, and anything you choose to tell us. We also record how you heard about us if you tell us. During a visit we may take photos of your pet and write grooming notes.

How we use it. To schedule and carry out appointments, keep your pet safe, contact you about a visit (for example, a reminder, a link to follow your pet's visit, a message that your pet is ready for pickup, or a visit summary with photos), take payment, and keep records so we can groom your pet well next time. We only send messages related to your appointments.

Who sees it. Only Sweet Beans Grooming. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising by others. We rely on a few service providers to run the business, such as our online scheduling, payment, email, website hosting, and website analytics services. They handle information only to provide their service to us. Each visit's private tracking link works only for whoever has that link, and it stops working some time after the visit.

Website analytics. Our website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, and we use Google Search Console to see how people find us. These reports are combined statistics about the website. We use a private, read-only tool to view our own website reports; it does not access any visitor's or customer's Google account.

How long we keep it. We keep client and pet records for as long as they are useful for your pet's care and for our business and tax records. Backups of our records are kept encrypted.

Your choices. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information, or to stop sending you email, at any time by contacting us below. We may keep limited records where the law or our accounting requires it.

Children. Our services and website are for adults; we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes. If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact. Questions or requests: Gary@sweetbeansgrooming.com or (206) 659-1768.

Located inside Ritchard's Grooming in Pioneer Square, at 306 4th Ave S, Seattle, WA 98104 (steps from SoDo and Pioneer Square Station light rail). Same address, separate one-on-one appointments.
